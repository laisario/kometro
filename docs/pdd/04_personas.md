# Personas

This document defines the primary user personas for KOMETROgo, representing the key stakeholders who interact with the platform.

---

## Persona 1: Marina Oliveira — Quality Manager

### Role
Quality Manager at a medium-sized pharmaceutical manufacturing company

### Background
Marina has 12 years of experience in quality management within regulated industries. She holds a degree in Chemical Engineering and certifications in ISO 9001 and ISO 17025. Her company operates three production facilities with over 800 measuring instruments ranging from precision balances to temperature sensors. She leads a team of four quality technicians responsible for maintaining metrological compliance across all sites.

### Goals
- Ensure 100% calibration compliance to pass regulatory audits without findings
- Reduce the time spent on manual tracking and reporting by at least 40%
- Demonstrate full traceability of instrument history to auditors and regulators
- Standardize calibration management processes across all three facilities
- Prevent production stoppages caused by expired or non-conforming instruments

### Pain Points
- Currently manages instruments using multiple Excel files that frequently become outdated
- Spends several hours each week manually checking calibration due dates
- Has no reliable way to prove audit trails during regulatory inspections
- Different facilities use inconsistent naming conventions and tracking methods
- Receives last-minute surprises when instruments are found expired during internal audits
- Struggles to coordinate with external calibration laboratories on scheduling

### How KOMETROgo Helps Marina
- Provides a single dashboard showing all instruments across facilities with real-time status
- Sends automatic email alerts 30 and 15 days before calibrations are due
- Maintains complete audit trails of all instrument movements and calibration history
- Enforces consistent tag naming and sector organization across the organization
- Enables quick filtering to identify non-conforming instruments before audits
- Stores all certificates digitally with easy retrieval for inspection purposes

## Persona 2: Ricardo Fernandes — Calibration Laboratory Technician

### Role
Senior Metrologist at B&F Laboratório de Metrologia (calibration service provider)

### Background
Ricardo has worked in calibration laboratories for 8 years, starting as a trainee and advancing to senior metrologist. He holds technical certifications in dimensional and pressure metrology. His daily work involves performing calibrations, issuing certificates, and maintaining the laboratory's reference standards. He processes approximately 50-70 instrument calibrations per month for various industrial clients.

### Goals
- Complete calibration work efficiently without administrative overhead
- Ensure all calibration records are accurate and certificates are properly issued
- Maintain organized records of work performed for each client
- Reduce errors in certificate generation and client communication
- Track which instruments from which clients are currently in the laboratory

### Pain Points
- Spends excessive time on paperwork instead of technical calibration work
- Occasionally loses track of which client instruments are in the lab awaiting calibration
- Manual certificate generation is error-prone and time-consuming
- Difficulty finding historical calibration data when clients request information
- No easy way to see a client's complete instrument portfolio when planning work
- Communication with clients about calibration status is mostly through individual emails

### How KOMETROgo Helps Ricardo
- Provides a clear view of all client instruments with their calibration requirements
- Tracks instrument position changes so he knows what's in the lab for calibration
- Stores calibration results and certificates in organized, searchable records
- Enables quick access to previous calibration history when performing repeat services
- Links calibration records to service orders for easy cross-reference
- Allows attaching multiple certificates and annexes to each calibration record

## Persona 3: Carla Mendes — Commercial Manager

### Role
Commercial Director at B&F Laboratório de Metrologia

### Background
Carla has 15 years of experience in B2B sales, the last 6 focused on technical services in the metrology sector. She manages a small commercial team responsible for new client acquisition, proposal generation, and client relationship management. Her role involves responding to quotation requests, negotiating contracts, and ensuring client satisfaction. She interacts with dozens of clients monthly, from small workshops to large industrial corporations.

### Goals
- Respond to quotation requests within 24 hours to stay competitive
- Generate accurate proposals that reflect current pricing and service offerings
- Maintain professional client communications with consistent branding
- Track proposal status and follow up on pending approvals effectively
- Increase conversion rate from proposals to confirmed service orders

### Pain Points
- Creating proposals manually takes 1-2 hours per request, limiting response capacity
- Pricing errors occur when copying from outdated price lists
- No centralized system to track which proposals are pending, approved, or lost
- Difficulty knowing a client's complete instrument inventory when preparing quotes
- Inconsistent proposal formats depending on who creates them
- Manual follow-up on pending proposals often falls through the cracks

### How KOMETROgo Helps Carla
- Generates professional PDF proposals in minutes by selecting instruments from client inventory
- Automatically calculates pricing based on current instrument catalog rates
- Tracks proposal status from draft through approval or rejection
- Maintains consistent proposal format with company branding
- Enables sending proposals directly via email from the platform
- Provides historical record of all proposals for each client relationship

## Persona 4: André Souza — Plant Maintenance Supervisor

### Role
Maintenance Supervisor at an automotive parts manufacturing plant

### Background
André has 20 years of experience in industrial maintenance, progressing from mechanic to supervisor. He manages a team of 15 maintenance technicians responsible for production equipment and associated measuring instruments. The plant has approximately 200 instruments under his responsibility, including torque wrenches, pressure gauges, and dimensional tools. He reports to the plant manager and coordinates with the quality department on instrument compliance.

### Goals
- Keep production running by ensuring instruments are always available and calibrated
- Minimize downtime caused by instrument issues or compliance gaps
- Organize instruments by production line and work area for easy tracking
- Respond quickly when the quality team identifies instrument issues
- Justify calibration budgets to management with clear data

### Pain Points
- Often discovers instruments are out of calibration only when quality flags them
- Difficult to locate instruments across a large manufacturing floor
- No visibility into which instruments are approaching calibration due dates
- Relies on the quality department to tell him what needs attention
- Cannot easily show management the status of instrument compliance
- Movement of instruments between areas is not tracked, causing confusion

### How KOMETROgo Helps André
- Provides dashboard view of all instruments organized by sector/production line
- Sends advance notifications before calibrations are due
- Tracks instrument positions (in use, in stock, in calibration) with movement history
- Shows clear status indicators (compliant, expiring soon, expired) at a glance
- Enables self-service access to instrument records without depending on quality team
- Offers exportable reports for management presentations on calibration status

## Persona 5: Juliana Costa — Document Control Specialist

### Role
Document Control Analyst at a food processing company

### Background
Juliana has 5 years of experience in quality systems documentation, with expertise in ISO 9001 and food safety standards (FSSC 22000). She is responsible for maintaining all controlled documents including procedures, work instructions, and forms. She coordinates document reviews with subject matter experts and ensures approvals are obtained before releasing new versions. The company maintains approximately 150 controlled documents.

### Goals
- Ensure all documents are reviewed and updated before their validity expires
- Streamline the approval process to reduce revision cycle times
- Maintain complete version history for audit purposes
- Notify document owners when reviews are due
- Eliminate unauthorized or obsolete document usage

### Pain Points
- Tracking document review dates across 150+ documents is overwhelming
- Chasing approvers for signatures delays document releases by days or weeks
- No automated notifications means manual calendar reminders that get missed
- Version control using file naming conventions leads to confusion
- Finding the correct current version during audits takes too long
- Paper-based approval routing is slow and creates storage challenges

### How KOMETROgo Helps Juliana
- Automatically tracks validity dates and sends review notifications
- Provides digital approval workflow with email notifications to approvers
- Maintains complete revision history with change descriptions
- Clearly indicates document status (valid, obsolete, cancelled)
- Enables quick search and retrieval of current document versions
- Stores all documents in cloud storage with secure access controls
