# Project Overview

## Project Name

**KOMETROgo** — Metrological Management System

## What is KOMETROgo?

KOMETROgo is a comprehensive SaaS platform designed to digitize and automate metrological management processes for industrial companies and calibration laboratories. The system provides a centralized environment where organizations can manage their entire fleet of measuring instruments, track calibration schedules, maintain document compliance, and generate commercial proposals.

## The Problem

Industrial organizations and calibration laboratories face significant challenges in managing their metrological assets:

- **Spreadsheet Chaos**: Instrument data scattered across multiple Excel files, leading to version conflicts and data loss
- **Missed Calibrations**: No automated alerts for upcoming or overdue calibrations, resulting in compliance violations and quality issues
- **Paper-Based Workflows**: Manual certificate handling, physical document storage, and time-consuming approval processes
- **Lack of Traceability**: Difficulty tracking instrument history, calibration results, and acceptance criteria over time
- **Inefficient Commercial Operations**: Manual proposal creation consuming hours of staff time with error-prone calculations
- **Compliance Risk**: Inability to demonstrate audit trails for regulatory standards (ISO 9001, ISO 17025, etc.)

## How KOMETROgo Helps Users

KOMETROgo eliminates these pain points by providing:

- **Centralized Instrument Registry**: A single source of truth for all measuring instruments with unique tags, serial numbers, and complete calibration history
- **Automated Alert System**: Email notifications sent at 30 days, 15 days, and on expiration date for calibrations and document reviews
- **Digital Document Management**: Version-controlled documents with multi-level approval workflows and secure cloud storage
- **Commercial Proposal Engine**: One-click PDF generation for calibration service proposals with automatic pricing calculations
- **Real-Time Dashboards**: Visual overview of instrument status, calibration compliance, and pending actions
- **Multi-Tenant Architecture**: Each client organization has isolated data with role-based access control

## High-Level Goals

1. **Eliminate Manual Tracking**: Replace spreadsheets and paper records with a modern digital platform
2. **Ensure Calibration Compliance**: Never miss a calibration due date through proactive automated notifications
3. **Streamline Document Workflows**: Reduce document revision and approval cycle times by 50%
4. **Accelerate Commercial Operations**: Generate professional proposals in minutes instead of hours
5. **Provide Full Traceability**: Maintain complete audit trails for all instrument movements, calibrations, and document changes
6. **Enable Data-Driven Decisions**: Offer dashboards and reports that surface actionable insights about metrological operations
7. **Support Regulatory Compliance**: Help organizations meet requirements for ISO 9001, ISO 17025, and industry-specific standards

## Non-Goals

KOMETROgo explicitly does **not** aim to:

1. **Replace Laboratory Information Management Systems (LIMS)**: The platform is not designed to manage laboratory samples, test results, or scientific workflows beyond calibration
2. **Perform Calibration Calculations**: The system tracks calibration results but does not calculate measurement uncertainties or perform metrological computations
3. **Manage Non-Metrological Equipment**: General asset management for IT equipment, vehicles, or facilities is out of scope
4. **Provide Financial/Accounting Features**: While proposals include pricing, invoicing, accounts receivable, and financial reporting are not included
5. **Offer On-Premise Deployment**: The platform is cloud-only (SaaS); self-hosted installations are not supported
6. **Support Real-Time Instrument Data Collection**: Direct integration with measurement devices or IoT sensors is not part of the current scope
7. **Handle Third-Party Calibration Vendor Management**: Managing external calibration service providers and their performance is outside the platform's focus
